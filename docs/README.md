# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This directory contains the process guidance used to plan, deliver, release, and continuously improve OctoAcme projects. Use the documents below as a shared reference for project teams and stakeholders.

## Project Management Process Summary

OctoAcme follows a lifecycle-based, iterative approach to project management. During **initiation**, teams validate the business need, define measurable goals and success criteria, identify stakeholders, capture initial risks, and decide whether the work should proceed to planning. During **planning**, an approved initiative is translated into a prioritized and estimated backlog with acceptance criteria, milestones, responsibilities, dependencies, a release plan, and a documented Definition of Done.

During **execution and tracking**, teams manage work on a project board, deliver in small increments, use pull requests with issue links and acceptance criteria, and rely on CI checks for tests and linting. Regular standups, delivery syncs, demos, and metrics keep progress visible. Risks and blockers are recorded in a risk register, reviewed regularly, and escalated from the team through the Project Manager and Product Lead to the sponsor when business impact requires it.

**Release and deployment** are controlled through acceptance checks, passing CI and security scans, release notes, smoke tests, post-deployment verification, and rollback or mitigation planning. After each sprint, release, milestone, or incident, teams conduct a **retrospective** to capture what went well, identify improvements, assign owners and due dates to action items, and measure their impact. Throughout the lifecycle, developers, Product Managers, and Project Managers collaborate on implementation, outcomes, prioritization, coordination, communication, and continuous improvement.
